# Code of Conduct

Last updated: September 11, 2026

## Our Commitment

We are committed to making participation in SMS Gateway OSS a welcoming, respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience, education, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, sex characteristics, sexual orientation, socioeconomic status, or technical background.

This commitment applies to maintainers, contributors, users, reviewers, issue reporters, and anyone participating in project spaces.

## Expected Behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive community include:

- Communicating respectfully and constructively.
- Giving and accepting technical feedback in good faith.
- Focusing criticism on code, design, documentation, or behavior—not on people.
- Respecting differing viewpoints and levels of experience.
- Clearly disclosing material AI assistance when contributing generated code or documentation.
- Taking responsibility for submitted work, including AI-assisted work.
- Protecting credentials, private data, phone numbers, SMS content, and security reports.
- Using the project only with devices, SIM cards, recipients, and systems you are authorized to use.
- Acknowledging mistakes, correcting them, and learning from them.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes, but is not limited to:

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, stalking, or discrimination.
- Insults, personal attacks, trolling, or deliberately inflammatory comments.
- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing another person’s private information without explicit permission.
- Sharing bearer tokens, private keys, signing keys, phone numbers, private messages, or vulnerability details without authorization.
- Encouraging spam, phishing, harassment, unsolicited bulk messaging, carrier abuse, or illegal activity.
- Submitting malicious code, hidden telemetry, credential theft, backdoors, or intentionally misleading changes.
- Impersonating maintainers, contributors, organizations, or project representatives.
- Repeatedly disrupting discussions, reviews, issues, or project operations.
- Retaliating against someone who reports a concern in good faith.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all official project spaces, including:

- GitHub issues and discussions.
- Pull requests and code reviews.
- Security reports.
- Project-related email and chat.
- Documentation and release notes.
- Public spaces where someone is acting as an official project representative.

It also applies when project participation directly affects the safety or well-being of community members.

## Reporting Conduct Concerns

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior privately to:

```text
[Insert Maintainer Conduct Email]
```

If a report concerns a security vulnerability rather than community conduct, follow `SECURITY.md` instead.

Do not include sensitive personal information in a public GitHub issue. When reporting a concern, provide only the information necessary to understand the incident, such as:

- What happened.
- Where and when it happened.
- Relevant links or screenshots.
- Whether the behavior is ongoing.
- Any immediate safety or privacy concerns.

Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible. Information may be shared only with people needed to review and resolve the matter, or when disclosure is legally required.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing acceptable behavior. They may edit, hide, reject, or remove comments, commits, code, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

Maintainers should:

- Review reports impartially and in good faith.
- Avoid conflicts of interest where practical.
- Protect reporter and subject privacy as reasonably possible.
- Document significant enforcement decisions.
- Apply consequences proportionately and consistently.
- Recuse themselves when they are directly involved in a reported incident.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Depending on context, severity, history, and impact, maintainers may take one or more of the following actions.

### 1. Correction

For minor or unintentional behavior, a maintainer may provide a private or public reminder and request that the behavior or content be corrected.

### 2. Warning

For a more serious incident or repeated minor violations, a formal warning may be issued. The participant may be asked to avoid specified interactions or behavior for a defined period.

### 3. Temporary Restriction

For sustained, disruptive, or harmful behavior, participation may be temporarily restricted. This can include locking discussions, limiting contact, closing contributions, or temporarily blocking an account.

### 4. Permanent Removal

For severe harassment, threats, deliberate abuse, malicious code, credential theft, illegal activity, repeated violations, or refusal to respect prior enforcement, a participant may be permanently removed from project spaces.

## Appeals

A person affected by an enforcement decision may submit a concise appeal through the private conduct-reporting contact. The appeal should explain why the decision should be reconsidered and include any relevant context not previously reviewed.

An appeal does not guarantee reversal. Where practical, it should be reviewed by a maintainer who was not directly responsible for the original decision.

## Good-Faith Technical Disagreement

Respectful disagreement about architecture, security, licensing, implementation, or project direction is not a Code of Conduct violation. Maintainers may still close or decline a proposal based on scope, risk, maintainability, or project priorities.

## No Authorization for Misuse

Nothing in this Code of Conduct authorizes testing or activity that would otherwise be unlawful or unauthorized. In particular, participation in this project does not grant permission to test against third-party devices, networks, mobile carriers, tunnel providers, or recipients.
